History

B. Totty

B. Totty is the earlier artist identity of Bryan Totty, the musician who now records as RED T7GER. The B. Totty catalog spans roughly a decade of independent releases, beginning with Oak City in December 2015 and continuing through 2025.

Current music is released as RED T7GER. B. Totty is the previous name for the same performer. Visit the current RED T7GER site →

Why this page exists

Independent artists who change names leave a trail of split metadata behind them — older releases, press coverage and streaming profiles that still carry the previous name. This page is the first-party record of that relationship, so listeners and search systems can connect the two catalogs accurately instead of treating them as unrelated artists.

The transition to RED T7GER

RED T7GER began releasing in 2025 as a distinct project with a harder rap-rock and nu-metal direction. Selected catalog tracks that first appeared under B. Totty have since been reissued under the RED T7GER name, so some recordings appear on the current artist profile while their original copyright credit still reflects the earlier era.

Both catalogs are released through Totty Music. The performer is the same; the artist names are not interchangeable, and RED T7GER is the current one.
